# Moving text, with time to think

[English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

## English

Words Actually Dance presents your own material as short animated sentences and keywords. It is a presentation tool for reading and review. **Controlled studies have not established this app's learning benefits: watching longer or enjoying its animation does not demonstrate better memory.** This guide separates research findings, actions you can try now, and features that still need development and evaluation.

The interface and nine built-in examples have Chinese and English versions. Imported notes keep their original wording: changing the interface language neither translates your material nor sends it to an AI service.

### Try three small actions

1. **Recall, then check.** After a short passage, pause, look away, and explain one idea in your own words. Revisit the source to correct mistakes and fill gaps.
2. **Return at another time.** Try recalling it later instead of only replaying it immediately. Adjust the interval to the material, your recall, and how long you need to remember it.
3. **Keep one point in view.** Understand one concept before continuing. Pause, revisit, or slow down if needed. If music distracts you, lower its volume or try silence; you can also reduce movement or turn off the ghost layer.

For example, after reading “Evaporation is the process in which liquid becomes gas at its surface,” explain it without looking. Check whether you included “at its surface,” then try recalling it at another time. This illustrates a practice method, not an experimental result obtained with this app.

### Why try these actions?

#### Retrieval practice: produce an answer yourself

Recognizing a visible answer and recalling it without looking are different experiences. In Roediger and Karpicke's reading experiments, recall practice outperformed an equal number of restudy opportunities on delayed tests; restudy sometimes did better after a very short delay. Add genuine recall to reading. Watching subtitles, marking something as seen, or replaying it does not automatically constitute retrieval practice. [Original study: Roediger & Karpicke, 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16507066/)

These findings concern particular materials and tests. A separate science-text experiment included comprehension and inference questions, but still does not establish equivalent benefits for this app, every knowledge domain, or arbitrary animations. [Karpicke & Blunt, 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21252317/) Checking your answer against the source is a practical suggestion; the software does not judge whether your answer is correct.

#### Spacing: distribute practice across time

Cepeda and colleagues' factual-learning experiment found that the useful review interval depended on the intended retention period; a longer gap was not always better. Start by learning something and recalling it at another time, then adjust to your recall. “Tomorrow, then a few days later” is an example schedule, not a universal optimum. Continuous replay is not spaced practice across separate occasions. [Original study: Cepeda et al., 2008](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2008.02209.x)

A broader quantitative review also considered practice intervals together with retention intervals, mainly in verbal-recall tasks. [Authors' review: Cepeda et al., 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16719566/)

#### Segmenting: control the pace of processing

Mayer and Chandler studied learner-controlled segments of a narrated animation about lightning. Some conditions improved transfer performance without a corresponding retention advantage. This motivates studying opportunities to process manageable segments; it does not establish that shorter videos or slower character-by-character playback are always better. [Original paper, university-held copy: Mayer & Chandler, 2001](https://tecfa.unige.ch/tecfa/teaching/methodo/Mayer_Chandler01.pdf)

This project offers short sentences, pause, revisit, and speed controls. Its materials and procedures differ from those experiments, so any learning benefit requires separate evaluation.

#### Signaling: clarify relationships, rather than emphasize everything

Mautone and Mayer used summaries, headings, and relational words to signal lesson structure without adding knowledge content. Learners performed better on transfer questions. A related design direction is to make important ideas and relationships clear; making every word larger or flashing is not equivalent to the studied intervention. [Original paper DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.93.2.377), [author-provided paper page](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/232494695_Signaling_as_a_Cognitive_Guide_in_Multimedia_Learning)

#### Working memory: simultaneous processing has limits

Understanding a sentence involves temporarily retaining earlier information and connecting it to what follows. Working-memory performance is limited and varies with the task, chunking, and prior knowledge. An experimental capacity estimate is not a fixed personal “brain capacity.” Breaking up steps, keeping the current point visible, or writing down pending tasks are approaches to try. [Cowan, 2001: theoretical and evidence discussion, author's laboratory copy](https://memory.psych.missouri.edu/assets/doc/articles/2001/cowan-bbs-2001.pdf)

#### Background music: enjoyment does not establish better learning

Lehmann and Seufert studied particular instrumental music and visually presented text. They found no general recall benefit; comprehension results interacted with measured working-memory capacity, with limitations involving small subgroups and the materials. This cannot determine whether an individual, a particular DJ track, or every learning task will benefit or suffer. [Original study: Lehmann & Seufert, 2017](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.01902/full)

Music is optional. For difficult material, compare how accurately you can explain it with music and in silence, not only which experience you prefer. Animation can also distract. No study has established that this project's beat matching, ghost layer, BPM, or particular text effects improve learning performance.

### Research and current feature boundaries

| Available now                                              | What it provides                                                                                     | What it does not establish                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Your text, TXT or standard script JSON, and local examples | Content control, sentence-by-sentence presentation, import and export                                | Automatically accurate content, expert review, or an integrated AI service |
| Pause, revisit, reveal speed, and motion settings          | Space for active recall, checking, and reading                                                       | Automatic knowledge of mastery or a measurement of your cognitive capacity |
| Recall and Show answer                                     | A pause and temporary concealment of the explanation; you decide when to continue after revealing it | Automatic grading, scoring, or proof that this interface improves memory   |
| Emphasis, keyword relationships, and separate body text    | Structural and visual cues                                                                           | Validation of every animation as an instructional intervention             |
| Music-driven text and a ghost-layer switch                 | Audio response and an adjustable experience                                                          | Guaranteed benefits from music, or viewing time as evidence of learning    |
| Three learning-method examples                             | Original demonstrations with Chinese and English versions                                            | Automatic quizzes, grading, reminders, or a spaced-review scheduler        |

Here, recall means producing an answer before consulting the source. Pausing, concealing the explanation, and revealing it are aids to that action. Showing the answer does not grade it or automatically resume playback.

Device-specific feature acceptance is recorded with the relevant release. Research cited on this page does not replace feature verification.

Answer feedback, cross-day review planning, and reminders could be developed separately. With user consent, future evaluations should compare delayed recall and application to new situations using comparable materials, study time, and conditions. Viewing duration, likes, and animation preference should not substitute for learning outcomes. These are future directions; automatic grading and review scheduling are not current services.

[English](#english)

### 让文字动起来，也给思考留时间

Words Actually Dance 把自己的材料变成随音乐呈现的短句和关键词。它是一种阅读与复习的呈现工具；**本软件的学习收益尚未经过对照研究验证，不能承诺看得更久、动画更吸引人就记得更牢。** 下面区分研究支持的学习方法、你现在可以做的事，以及未来还需要开发和验证的功能。

界面和九份内置示例均有中文、英文版本。导入的笔记保留原文：切换界面语言不会翻译你的材料，也不会把它发送给 AI 服务。

### 先试这三个小动作

1. **先回想，再核对。** 读完一小段就暂停，移开视线，用自己的话说出一个要点。再看原文，补上遗漏或纠正错误。
2. **换个时间再想。** 不必马上反复重播。之后再回忆一次；根据内容难度、想保留多久和实际回忆情况调整复习时间。
3. **一次抓一个重点。** 先读懂一个概念，再继续。跟不上时暂停、回看或减速；音乐分心时调低音量或改在安静环境阅读，也可降低舞动强度或关闭虚影。

例如，学完“蒸发是液体表面变成气体的过程”，先不看解释，自己说一次；核对有没有漏掉“液体表面”；之后换个时间再试。这个例子说明操作方法，不是本软件已经取得的实验结果。

### 为什么这样做

#### 检索练习：让自己把内容想出来

“看着答案很熟悉”和“离开答案还能说出来”是两种体验。Roediger 与 Karpicke 的阅读材料实验中，练习回忆在延迟测验上优于等次数重读；很短延迟的测验则出现过重读优势。因此，不必排斥阅读，而是给阅读增加一次真正的回忆。只看字幕、点“看过了”或循环播放，并不自动构成检索练习。[原始研究：Roediger & Karpicke, 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16507066/)

适用边界：这项研究使用特定材料和测验。另一项科学文本实验也研究了理解与推论题，仍不能直接推出本软件、所有知识类型或任意动画都有相同效果。[Karpicke & Blunt, 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21252317/) 这里的“再核对原文”是具体使用建议，不表示软件已能判断回答对错。

#### 间隔：把复习分散到不同时间

Cepeda 等人的事实学习实验表明，复习间隔与最终需要记住多久有关；间隔并非越长越好。可以从“今天学，之后另找时间回忆”开始，再根据回忆情况调整。“明天一次、几天后一次”只能作为安排示例，不是人人适用的最优公式。连续重播一篇内容，也不能当成跨时间的间隔练习。[原始研究：Cepeda et al., 2008](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2008.02209.x)

更广的定量综述也指出，练习间隔和保留时间需要一起考虑；其范围主要是言语回忆任务。[原作者综述：Cepeda et al., 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16719566/)

#### 分段：把处理的节奏交回自己

Mayer 与 Chandler 让学习者分段控制一段讲解闪电形成的动画。部分条件下，学习者在迁移题上表现更好，记忆题却没有同样的优势。这个结果支持研究“能否按自己的速度处理小段内容”，并不证明视频越短越好、逐字播放越慢越好。[原始论文，大学机构保存本：Mayer & Chandler, 2001](https://tecfa.unige.ch/tecfa/teaching/methodo/Mayer_Chandler01.pdf)

在本项目中，短句、暂停、回看与速度设置提供了可用的控制手段。它们与论文里的材料和流程不同，学习收益仍须另行检验。

#### 提示：让重要关系清楚，而非处处抢眼

Mautone 与 Mayer 的实验用摘要、标题和关系词等线索说明材料结构，帮助学习者回答迁移问题；提示没有增加新的知识内容。对应的设计方向是让重点和关系更清楚，不能把“所有文字都放大、闪动”当成同样的方法。[原始论文 DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.93.2.377)，[作者提供的论文页面](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/232494695_Signaling_as_a_Cognitive_Guide_in_Multimedia_Learning)

#### 工作记忆：同时处理的信息有上限

理解一句话时，要暂时保留前面的内容，再把它与后面联系起来。工作记忆容量有限，具体表现受任务、组块和已有知识等因素影响。不要把某个实验里的数量写成每个人固定的“脑容量”。把步骤拆开、保留当前重点、把待办写下来，是可以尝试的处理方式。[Cowan, 2001：理论与证据讨论，作者实验室保存本](https://memory.psych.missouri.edu/assets/doc/articles/2001/cowan-bbs-2001.pdf)

#### 背景音乐：喜欢听，不等于学得更好

Lehmann 与 Seufert 的实验使用特定无歌词音乐与视觉文本，没有发现普遍的回忆收益；理解表现与所测工作记忆水平之间存在交互，作者也指出小分组与材料的限制。不能据此判断某位用户、某首 DJ 或每种学习任务必然受益或受损。[原始研究：Lehmann & Seufert, 2017](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.01902/full)

音乐是本工具的可选体验。遇到难内容，比较自己在有音乐和安静环境下能否准确复述，而不只比较哪种更喜欢。动画同样可能分散注意力；没有研究证明本项目的卡点、虚影、BPM 或某种字效能提高学习成绩。

### 研究与当前功能的边界

| 你现在可以使用                          | 它实际提供什么                                         | 还不能据此声称                             |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| 自备文本、TXT／标准文案 JSON 和本地示例 | 控制内容、逐句呈现、导入导出                           | 内容自动正确、已经过专家审校或 AI 已接入   |
| 暂停、回看、显现速度与舞动设置          | 给主动回想、核对和阅读留出空间                         | 自动知道你是否学会，或已经测出个人认知能力 |
| “回想一下”与“看答案”                    | 暂停播放、暂时遮住讲解；揭示原文后仍由用户决定何时继续 | 自动判题、计分或已经证明这种界面能提高记忆 |
| 重点强调、关键词关系和独立正文          | 提供结构与视觉线索                                     | 每种动效都属于经过验证的教学干预           |
| 音乐驱动文字与虚影开关                  | 音频响应和可调体验                                     | 音乐必然增效、看得久就是学得好             |
| 三份学习方法示例                        | 原创演示，均有中文和英文版本                           | 已经运行自动测验、评分、提醒或间隔调度算法 |

这里的回想，是让自己先产生一个回答，再查看原文。暂停、暂时遮住讲解或揭示原文都只是操作辅助；揭示原文不等于自动判题，也不会自动继续播放。

功能在具体设备上的验收状态以对应版本记录为准；本页的研究依据不能代替功能验收。

未来可以单独开发答案反馈、跨日复习安排与提醒，并在用户同意的前提下评估延迟回忆和新情境应用。评估应比较相同材料、学习时间和条件，不能用停留时长、点赞数或动画偏好替代学习结果。这些是后续建议，当前没有自动评分或复习调度服务。
