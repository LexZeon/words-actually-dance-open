## English

The public demonstrations use this project's procedural demo music. The four third-party references below are listening and interaction references; their recordings, lyrics and private demonstrations containing source-song audio are not distributed with this repository, release packages or public demo videos. Attribution is not a licence or an endorsement.

The public previews corresponding to the linked songs are silent recordings of this project's own app interface and original knowledge text. They contain no source-song audio, Bilibili player imagery or song lyrics. Readers can open the song at its source link separately. Attribution and a silent preview do not grant permission to copy, synchronise or distribute the source song.

Credits checked on **2026-10-22 UTC**. Original performers, writers and video uploaders are separate roles; unresolved version credits remain explicitly unverified.

### Original procedural music

Created for Words Actually Dance by **LexZeon Studio and project contributors**, through AI-assisted development. The synthesizer, note patterns and percussion are generated in [demo-music.js](https://github.com/LexZeon/words-actually-dance-open/blob/main/src/audio/demo-music.js). No recordings, sampled vocals or melodies from the referenced songs are used.
